If you're an AI agent, or a human who wants to get started quickly with an AI agent, run the following prompt in your agent:


The AI agent will set up Band Store locally, deploy it to [Cloud](!cloud!), and help you build your custom commerce features.

## What is Band Store

Band Store is an AI-native commerce platform with a Framework to build custom commerce features.

Band Store ships with three main tools:

1. A suite of [Commerce Modules](!resources!/commerce-modules) with core commerce functionalities, such as tracking inventory, calculating cart totals, accepting payments, managing orders, and much more.
2. A [Framework](./fundamentals/framework/page.mdx) for building agentic flows and custom functionalities specific to your business, product, or industry. This includes tools for introducing custom API endpoints, business logic, and data models; building workflows and automations; and integrating with third-party services.
3. A customizable admin dashboard for merchants to configure and operate their store.

When you install Band Store, you get a fully fledged commerce platform with all the features you need to get off the ground. And unlike other platforms, Band Store doesn't require hacky workarounds that are difficult to maintain and scale.

Band Store is built with customization in mind. Build features that bring your business's vision to life, with native support for AI agents to automate your operations and customer interactions.

## Who should use Band Store

Band Store is for businesses and teams looking for a cutting-edge commerce platform for their unique requirements, visions, and automated workflows, which other platforms aren't built to support.

Businesses of all sizes can use Band Store, from small startups building with agentic flows to large enterprises building complex commerce features. Band Store is also for developers who want to build with the latest technologies and tools, and have the freedom to implement unique features without being limited by the platform's architecture.
