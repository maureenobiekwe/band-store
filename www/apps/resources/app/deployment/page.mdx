## Cloud

Cloud is our managed services offering for Band Store applications. Cloud hosts your server, Admin dashboard, database, and Redis instance. The infrastructure is security-compliant and optimized for Band Store and supports:

- Zero configuration.
- Built-in autoscaling.
- Deploy to regions around the world.
- 99.99% uptime guarantee.

With Cloud, you maintain full customization control as you deploy your own modules and customizations directly from GitHub:

- Push to deploy.
- Multiple testing environments.
- Preview environments for new PRs.
- Test on production-like data.
- Storefront deployment.
- Emails setup.
- [And more](!cloud!#cloud-features).

[Sign up and learn more about Cloud](!cloud!/sign-up).
